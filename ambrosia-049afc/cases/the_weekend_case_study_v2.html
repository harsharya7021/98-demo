The Setting
Is The
Story.

A constellation creator launch for The Weekend Wine & More — a Moroccan-inspired fine-dining and wine destination on the upper deck of Gardens Galleria Mall, Noida. Ten creators across four audience verticals. Combined reach 1.45M. One venue, one weekend, one cultural arrival.

01
The Snapshot

The Weekend Wine & More opened on the upper deck of Gardens Galleria Mall, Noida — a saturated F&B floor with established competition. The brief to 98 was not "promote a restaurant"; it was "make sure the city's content audiences walk through this venue's door before the algorithm decides what it is".

98 designed the launch as a constellation: ten creators chosen across four distinct audience verticals, each shooting the venue through their own grammar, all sharing a tag-stack and geo-pin discipline. The Moroccan room — teal chandeliers, floral wine-bottle installation, brass-tassel column lamps, deep imported-spirits cellar — was the consistent star.

02
The Challenge

Gardens Galleria already had an established F&B floor in mid-2022. The Weekend was the new tenant on a deck where every neighbouring window already had foot traffic, regular guests, and an Instagram tag stack. A maximalist Moroccan wine bar opening into that ecosystem could not announce itself with a hoarding or a Zomato listing — both would read as "another opening". It had to enter the city's content stream as a place that was already a story.

  • The setting is the proposition. The Weekend isn't selling pasta or pinot. It's selling a maximalist Arab-Mediterranean drawing room. That world doesn't photograph through stock-photo restaurant content. It needs creators whose feeds reward setting, not bites.
  • Three audiences, one window. The launch had to land with food creators (saves, recipes), aesthetic / lifestyle creators (saved as inspo), and fashion / portrait creators (saved as a backdrop). Three different feeds, one restaurant, one three-week window before the algorithm decided what the venue is.
  • A saturated mall floor. Discovery on Gardens Galleria's geo-pin is contested. Without a coordinated tag-stack policy across creators, every visit would have leaked into a different cluster of searches.
  • Asset compounding for after the window. A new venue's first month sets the tone for its first quarter. The launch had to leave the brand with creative it could keep using — for Diwali service, anniversary covers, menu drops.
03
The Strategic Move

Don't sell the menu.
Build a constellation.

A single hero creator post would have been an ad. Three would have been a campaign. We chose ten, mapped across four audience pools, each picked for the specific audience moment they could capture inside the room. Each creator's content reads as their own; the consistent grammar (the Moroccan setting, the tag stack, the geo-pin) does the work of making them feel like a chorus.

01
Mix the verticals

Food creators alone would have made the launch feel like a soft opening. Lifestyle alone would have felt like a vibe-piece. The combination — food + design / aesthetic + fashion / portrait + couples / luxury — made the launch read as cultural, not commercial.

02
Mix the reach tiers

Verified Tier-1 (Sonali Arora, Mandeep Singh) carried the broadcast layer. Mid-tier (Veronica, Sara Malhotra, Anchit & Surabhi) carried weekend-bookings intent. Niche-but-deep (Sukriti the architect, Stillsbyayushjain the F&B photographer) carried credibility — different leverage from the same constellation.

03
One tag-stack discipline

Same handle pair on every visit (@theweekendwineandmore + @98.entertainment), founder re-share via @harsh.aryaa, geo-tag locked to Gardens Galleria. Ten creators behaved like one source for the location pin's algorithm.

What the Work Delivered

Reach & Discovery

  • 1.45M combined follower reach across ten creators — layered across food, lifestyle, fashion, design and luxury-couples verticals.
  • 4 verified broadcast handles (@sonaliarora__, @mandeepgujjar_, @shatakshitomar, @_sukku_) carrying the algorithmic-trust layer.
  • Standardised geo-tag drove search-ability of "Gardens Galleria · The Weekend" on Instagram's location pin during the critical first three weeks.

Creative Quality

  • Ten creator stories that read as a curated collection — Moorish archways, chandeliers, the floral wine-bottle wall, the whisky shelving — not a series of reshoots.
  • Format range across single Reels, 4-up Story collages, dessert close-ups, full-length portraits and a brand-curated portrait shoot — every grid behaviour catered to.
  • One photographer-grade still (@stillsbyayushjain) usable as evergreen launch creative.

Booking & Behaviour

  • Save-content behaviour was the goal — creators in this roster over-index on saves, so the launch fed weekend reservation intent rather than one-night impressions.
  • The floral installation became a self-perpetuating selfie pin — every subsequent guest now photographs the same wall.
  • Couples-occasion positioning seeded via @food_travel_etc — anniversary, milestone and date-dinner intent baked into the venue's brand by Week 3.

Asset Longevity

  • Brand-curated portrait shoot still in rotation as evergreen launch creative — same tagging convention, indistinguishable from organic.
  • Creator roster scoped during launch is reusable for subsequent activations (Diwali, anniversary, new menu drops, Christmas service).
  • Tag-stack template & geo-pin discipline now operationalised — the venue knows how to brief any future content visit.

Read it together and the constellation works because each creator brought a different value class. The verifieds carried the broadcast; the architect carried the credibility; the photographer carried the asset; the aesthetic creator carried the saves; the couples carried the LTV; the fashion creator carried the selfie pin. Same room. Ten angles. One launch.

The Weekend engagement proves a launch playbook that transfers cleanly to any restaurant, lounge, café or experiential venue opening in NCR with a strong physical identity. The capability stack is the asset.

Constellation roster, not single hero
