Movement.

98's largest creator activation by headcount. 58 creators across 88 story frames at Slay — coffee + everyday lifestyle brand. Combined reach 6.92M (22 verified handles). Anchored on Ammy Arora (1.09M, public figure), Syma Verma (766K, reel creator), Saloni Sehra (747K, reel creator).

01
The Snapshot

Slay is a coffee + everyday-lifestyle brand whose marketing problem is the inverse of a fine-dining launch. The product travels in moments, not destinations — coffee in the car, an outfit-of-the-day with the can on the table, a UGC clip in the morning routine. The brief to 98 was to make the can appear in 50+ places at the same time.

98 stacked the largest creator headcount in our portfolio: 58 active creators across 88 captured story frames. Anchored on three mega creators above 700K (Ammy Arora 1.09M, Syma Verma 766K, Saloni Sehra 747K), with 22 verified voices across the stack. The roster covers reel creators, lifestyle, UGC, makeup artists, artists, and bloggers — every audience class where the can might travel.

02
The Challenge

An everyday product needs to appear in everyday content — not in launch-event content. That requires a creator stack that does its own thing, with the product folded in. 58 creators is not 'one brief with 58 voices'; it's 58 creators each doing their own grammar with one shared product anchor.

  • Volume that reads as ambient. If 58 creators all post the same week, it reads as paid. Spread across multiple weeks, it reads as 'this thing is everywhere'.
  • Diversity over uniformity. The roster spans reel creators, makeup artists, UGC voices, lifestyle creators, bloggers, artists. Each creator brings a different audience class — same product, eight different shelf placements.
  • Mega anchors + long tail. Three creators above 700K stop the audience from filing the campaign as 'tiny influencers'. The long tail of 50+ smaller voices does the saturation work.
  • Format flexibility. Slay's brief allowed reels, stories, hauls, OOTDs, and integrations. Most brands lock format. Slay let creators bring their format to the product.
03
The Strategic Move

Volume done badly looks paid.
Volume done right
looks ambient.

01
Three-mega anchor.

Ammy Arora 1.09M + Syma Verma 766K + Saloni Sehra 747K. Three reel creators above 700K signal 'this is a moment', not 'a small brand seeding'.

02
Format-flexible briefs.

Each creator picked the format that fit their feed. Slay travelled across reels, stories, OOTDs, and UGC clips — natural placement, no campaign markers.

03
Verified saturation (22).

22 verified voices in one campaign is unusual. The verified density signals trust at a scale most brands can't book.

What Delivered

The Slay · coffee + everyday lifestyle brand campaign produced four distinct asset classes — built to live beyond the launch window and re-run on the brand's owned channels.

Reels

  • Multi-creator reel set across 88 captured story frames.
  • Each creator in their native format.
  • No locked audio bed — creators chose their own.

Stories

  • 88 story frames captured.
  • Spread across multiple weeks for ambient saturation.

Verified Density

  • 22 verified handles in a single activation.
  • Near-impossible to replicate in one window.

Brand-As-Pattern

  • Slay can travel as background to creator content rather than focus.
  • Asset library handed to Slay for owned channels.
08
98 · The Capability

Slay is 98's case for ambient saturation done with discipline. Pick the right anchors so the campaign doesn't read as small, then let the long tail do the saturation work. Format-flexibility is the trick that makes 58 creators feel like 58 friends recommending the same thing.
