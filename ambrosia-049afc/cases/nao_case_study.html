02
The Challenge

Berco's is a 30-year-old Indo-Chinese institution. Nao is a contemporary pan-Asian concept — different cuisine, different room, different audience. The launch had to leverage Berco's distribution and operational infrastructure without inheriting its menu equity. Diners had to read Nao as a new brand — recognisable, but not extension.

  • Spinoff trap. Most parent-brand spinoffs read as 'lite version of the original'. Nao had to land as a peer concept, not a child concept.
  • Mega-anchor signal. Ammy Arora (1.09M) and Shreya Chadda (480K) signal 'destination' on day one. Without them, Nao would have read as 'new outlet of Berco's'.
  • Tagline lock. 'Live in the Nao' had to enter IG search as a recognisable phrase — every creator caption ended the same way.
  • Cross-format coverage. Couple-duo (street-style), single mega creators, food creators — three formats covering the room from three angles.
