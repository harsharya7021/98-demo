After Dark
In GK Two.

A 14-creator constellation launch for Deja Brew — a Greater Kailash 2 café reframed as Delhi's preferred 8 PM sufi & sheesha room. Combined reach 3.65M, 10 verified handles, 27 story frames, four-week launch window.

01
The Snapshot

Deja Brew sits in Greater Kailash 2 — a market with a café on every corner. The brief to 98 was specific: own a particular evening (sufi · sheesha · soft-lit social), not a daytime brunch slot. Convert a daytime café into the city's 8 PM spot for the fashion + UGC crowd inside a four-week window.

98 stacked a constellation: 14 creators across 39K to 532K followers, ten verified, a deliberate range that prevents single-tier saturation. The roster mixed fashion-creators (Saanya Khatter, Prakriti Saha, Ishita Anand, Ayushi Singh), film + comedy voices (Sid Gulati of @wokenupsid_, Hardik Arora), and entrepreneurs (Aashkara Bidani) — not just Instagram tiles, but a cross-vertical pull that read as 'everyone in your feed is here'.

02
The Challenge

GK 2 has a café on every corner, and most run on a daytime grammar — brunch, oat-milk lattes, work-from-here. Deja Brew needed to own a different time of day: 8 PM and after, sufi-loop bed, sheesha-friendly, room-as-living-room. The risk wasn't 'will people hear about it' — it was 'will they file it correctly when they do'.

  • The 8 PM problem. A daytime café's evening positioning doesn't change because the venue says so — it changes because the city's content stream associates the room with evening. That requires evening-only content.
  • Constellation, not anchor. One mega creator can't sell 'everyone-you-follow-is-going-there'. We needed 14 creators across audience classes for the social proof to compound.
  • Tier diversity. If all 14 creators were 400K+, the campaign would read as paid. If all were 60K, reach would underperform. The mix had to span 39K → 532K to read as natural rotation.
  • Cross-vertical lift. Fashion creators alone would have stacked one shelf. Film (Sid Gulati) and comedy (Hardik Arora) opened the venue to audiences fashion creators don't reach.
