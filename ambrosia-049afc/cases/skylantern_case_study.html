Sky Lantern is a courtyard café in Panchsheel Park — the kind of room the city likes but doesn't have to seek out. The brief to 98 was to take a Sunday luncheon and turn it into a feed-event: not 'come for lunch', but 'this is where Delhi's content audience is on a Sunday afternoon in winter'.

02
The Challenge

Courtyard cafés in Delhi face a discovery problem: they don't have walk-by traffic, and the room reads better at certain times than others. Sky Lantern needed to convert a one-day Sunday service into ambient cultural recognition. That requires a creator stack that posts in tandem, around a single calendar anchor, with the same editorial mood.

  • Calendar anchoring. A single Sunday luncheon, branded as the 'Sky Sunday Luncheon · Winter Edition', gave the campaign a referential moment instead of a generic visit window.
  • Editorial mood as positioning. 'Delhi Winters are not a season. They are a celebration' became the campaign tagline — printed on the invite, echoed across captions, locking the room's mood to the Delhi-winter season.
  • Volume as cultural signal. 70 story frames across 22 creators inside a tight window read as 'this is where everyone is' — the ambient saturation play, applied to a courtyard.
  • Multi-class verified stack. 13 verified handles spanning public figures, fashion models, lifestyle, and digital creators. Sky Lantern read as a destination across four audience classes simultaneously.
