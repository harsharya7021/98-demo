02
The Challenge

Delhi's premium fusion shelf is contested — diners arrive with strong priors about which Italian and which Levantine they trust. RIZQ's plating was its primary asset, but plating doesn't compound without the right vocabulary — and that vocabulary doesn't come from food creators alone. It comes from actors, models, fashion voices whose audiences file restaurants as destinations.

  • Fusion is a positioning trap. Diners default-suspect fusion concepts because most are dilution. The campaign had to lead with plating that read as conviction, not compromise.
  • Wrong creator class = wrong shelf. If we had stacked food creators, RIZQ would have landed on a deal-and-discount shelf. Actors, models, and luxury creators put it on a destination shelf.
  • Volume that reads as natural. 94 story frames over the launch window — engineered to read as 'this is where everyone is', not 'this is a launch'.
  • Asset compounding for six months. Premium openings have a six-month tail. The shoot delivered content that lived on RIZQ's grid through their first festive cycle.
