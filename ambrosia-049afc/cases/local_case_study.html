02
The Challenge

Noida sits in a strange creator-marketing position — large addressable population, plenty of disposable income, but most creator rosters live across the river. A campaign that ships in Delhi voices reads as 'visiting'. A campaign that uses Noida voices reads as 'belongs'. Local needed the second read.

  • Geography is grammar. When Delhi creators visit Noida, their followers read 'crossed the river'. When Noida-or-near creators post about Noida, their followers read 'this is around here'.
  • Weekend window discipline. Local trades on Friday and Saturday night. Mid-week reels lose 60% of conversion. The launch ran on a strict weekend-only publication grid.
  • Audio brand. ROSALÍA's late-night, Latin-pop sound matched the venue's energy. We locked it across the stack — the story tray became a single audio brand.
  • Verified blogger anchor. Akanksha Upneja's verified blogger status gave Local credibility — the kind of cover most Noida campaigns can't book.
