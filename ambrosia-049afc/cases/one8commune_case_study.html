The Snapshot

One8 Commune is Virat Kohli's lifestyle brand — a venue category where the founder is already the headline. The risk is the inverse of most launches: the brand is so named that creators read it as 'brand-tour content' rather than 'place-I-go content'. The brief to 98 was to make the room feel personal, not promotional.

02
The Challenge

Celebrity-anchored brands have a specific creator-marketing problem: every visit risks reading as 'a tour of the celebrity's place'. To get One8 Commune to land as a venue rather than a celebrity-tie-in, the creators had to shoot the room as if the celebrity wasn't part of the equation. Bring their own grammar. Let the room speak for itself.

  • Founder-as-headline trap. When the founder is the headline, every creator visit risks reading as 'visiting the celebrity's place'. Need creators who'll lead with their own POV.
  • Frame depth without launch-spike. 98 story frames is a saturation level most brands can't sustain. Stretched across multiple weeks to read as 'always-on'.
  • Cross-format coverage. Actor anchor + reel creators + lifestyle + film + comedy. Each format handles the room differently.
  • Verified anchor stack. 18 verified handles inside one venue activation — credibility scaffolding.
