The Brief

Punjab Grill is the established premium-Indian play — banqueting-credible, multi-outlet, the kind of name diners remember from every important family dinner. The creator brief: stop the brand from reading "older" by anchoring it in occasion-led content rather than category-led content.

02
The Strategic Move

Don't sell "north Indian food." Sell the room your family last booked for an occasion. We worked the brand into a creator's life-event content — date night, family dinner, anniversary, post-engagement bite — letting the brand become the implicit answer when the audience asks where.

  • Occasion-led, not category-led. Premium Indian is a contested shelf; the win comes from owning the family-occasion shelf instead.
  • Younger creators, family vocabulary. Cast for creators whose own audience treats restaurants as life-event venues.
  • Multi-outlet calendar. Spread visits across the chain's outlets, not concentrated in one — turning the brand's footprint into part of the campaign.

04
What This Proved

Occasion beats category at the established-brand tier. Premium Indian is contested. The family-occasion shelf is uncontested. We picked the second one.

Multi-outlet activation = brand-footprint as content. The brand's outlet count became part of the creative — visits spread across outlets gave the campaign visible scale.

The daughter post lifts the brand without breaking authority. Younger creator content delivered against an older brand promise — the trust held.
