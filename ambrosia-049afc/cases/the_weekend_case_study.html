01
The Snapshot

The Weekend Wine & More — a maximalist Moroccan-inspired fine-dining and wine destination opening on the upper level of Gardens Galleria Mall, Noida — partnered with 98 Entertainment for its launch communications. The brief: don't run an ad campaign for a new restaurant. Run a cultural arrival — a steady drumbeat of creator-led arrivals that each one of the city's content audiences would recognise.

98 ran the launch end-to-end across food, lifestyle, design and fashion creator pools — venue bookings and table assignments, creator visit choreography, content briefs that protected the venue's photo-first aesthetic, brand-safe tag stacks, and a brand-curated portrait shoot that ran alongside the organic UGC.

02
The Challenge

Noida — and Gardens Galleria specifically — already had a saturated F&B floor in mid-2022. A fine-dining wine bar opening in that footprint had to do more than announce hours and a menu. The Weekend wasn't selling pasta or wine; it was selling a world — an Arab-Mediterranean drawing room of brass tassels, teal Moroccan chandeliers, ornate floral installations and a deep imported-spirits cellar. That world is hard to convey in a hoarding or a Zomato listing.

  • The setting is the proposition. A creator dinner at The Weekend was already a story before the food arrived. We had to build a launch programme that let the venue photograph itself, through eyes the audience trusted.
  • Three audiences, one window. The opening had to land with food creators (saves, recipes), lifestyle / aesthetic creators (saved as inspo), and fashion / portrait creators (saved as a backdrop for their own work). Three different feeds, one restaurant.
  • No first-week silence. A new venue's first 21 days set the algorithm and the table-booking pattern. The launch had to be a continuous, layered drumbeat — not a single hero post.
03
The Strategic Move

Don't sell the menu.
Send the audience
to a place.

We reframed the launch around arrival — every creator's content had to feel like a postcard from somewhere new, not a paid restaurant review. The brief to creators was short: visit the venue, eat what you'd actually order, shoot what you'd actually shoot, tag us. Production value emerged from the room itself; we just made sure enough creators were in the room.

01
The room as the hero

Every visit was choreographed to put the venue's signature furniture in frame — the teal Moorish chandeliers, the brass-tassel column lamps, the floral & wine-bottle installation behind the bar. We filtered creators whose feeds reward setting over product, then let them shoot freely.

02
Three creator verticals, one tag stack

Food (Sonali Arora, Shatakshi Tomar, Veronica), aesthetic / lifestyle (Legallypinteresty, Aanchal), and fashion / portrait (Sara Malhotra) — the same restaurant, three audiences. Standardised tag stack (@theweekendwineandmore + @98.entertainment + Gardens Galleria geo-tag) compounded the venue's location pin.

03
Brand-curated, organic-shaped

Alongside organic UGC, 98 ran a small brand-side portrait shoot at the floral installation that was visually indistinguishable from the creator content — same floral wall, same cocktail, same tagging convention — extending the launch with assets the brand owns outright.

07
How It Was Run

A venue launch is logistics first, creative second. The Weekend's first three weeks could not afford an empty Friday. 98 held the venue, the F&B team and the creator roster on one continuous schedule.

Stage What 98 Owned Why It Mattered
Creative strategy Three-vertical creator architecture (food / aesthetic / fashion); standardised tag stack; Gardens Galleria geo-tag policy. One launch served three feeds without diluting the venue voice on any of them.
Creator sourcing Vetting for feed-fit (does this creator's grid reward setting?), commercial benchmarking, regional weighting toward Delhi NCR audiences. Filtered out review-bloggers; locked in creators whose content makes a venue feel destination, not transaction.
Visit choreography Booking the right table, time of day for chandelier light, dish selection that read well on camera, two creators max per evening to protect the room. Each visit produced photographable content without turning the floor into a press junket.
Brand-safe tagging Tag stack templates, hashtag templates, geo-pin checks before posting. Brand-side @harsh.aryaa add for founder-network re-share. Every story added to the venue's geo-pin's discoverability — a single launch behaved like dozens of small ones.
Brand-curated content One short brand-side portrait shoot at the floral installation, intentionally styled to read as creator content. Gave the brand its own evergreen launch assets without breaking the organic feel of the campaign.
Cadence management Posting schedule across the three-week launch window so a new story appeared at the venue's geo-pin every 2–3 days. The opening drumbeat never went silent; the algorithm learned this venue was a place people kept arriving at.
08
What the Work Delivered

Creative Quality

  • Ten+ creator stories that read as a curated collection — Moorish archways, chandeliers, the floral wall, the whisky shelving — not a series of reshoots.
  • Visual consistency without forcing a template: every creator brought their own voice; the room kept the brand voice consistent for them.

Reach & Discovery

  • Combined creator audience layered across food, lifestyle and fashion verticals — three Delhi-NCR pools that don't normally see the same restaurant content.
  • Standardised geo-tag drove search-ability of "Gardens Galleria · The Weekend" on Instagram's location pin during the critical first three weeks.

Booking & Behaviour

  • Saved-content behaviour was the goal — creators in this roster over-index on saves (recipe / inspo / outfit-location), so the launch fed weekend reservation intent rather than one-night impressions.
  • The floral installation became a self-perpetuating selfie pin — the kind of room guests photograph for their own grid.

Asset Longevity

  • Brand-curated portrait shoot still in rotation as evergreen launch creative — same tagging convention, indistinguishable from organic.
  • Creator roster scoped during launch is reusable for subsequent activations (rakhi specials, Christmas service, anniversary, new menu drops).
