The Snapshot

Hard Rock Cafe doesn't need an introduction in Delhi. The brand is iconic — Janpath outlet, music-led grammar, decades of cultural recognition. The brief to 98 was the inverse of a launch: don't introduce, reassert. A heritage music brand needed feed-presence calibrated to its existing weight, not promotional volume.

98 ran a tight, intentional stack of 4 creators across 8 story frames — the smallest activation in our portfolio by volume, and deliberately so. Harteerath Singh Ahluwalia (297K verified, founder of Hemkunt Foundation) anchored the founder-class lift; Suhani Dhawan (70K verified) and Urnisha Swargari (57K verified) brought cross-format coverage; Kashish Taneja (39K) extended the bench. The campaign signal was 'Hard Rock is on the Delhi creator agenda', not 'Hard Rock is launching'.

02
The Challenge

Heritage brands have a specific creator-marketing problem: a high-volume creator stack risks reading as a re-launch, which can flatten the brand's existing equity. The campaign had to be small, controlled, and credibility-led. One founder-class anchor + three verified voices is the pattern that says 'we belong here', not 'please notice us'.

  • Heritage equity preservation. Hard Rock's brand grammar is iconic. A 20+ creator volume push would have read as 'trying'. Four creators reads as 'natural'.
  • Founder-class anchor. Harteerath Singh Ahluwalia's philanthropist + author profile lifts the venue out of the 'restaurant being marketed' shelf and into the 'place where serious people go' shelf.
  • Music-night format. The 8 January 'Rock The Night Away' invite anchored the campaign on the music-led identity that's central to Hard Rock's brand.
  • Verified density at small volume. 3 of 4 creators verified — a 75% verification rate that signals institutional credibility even at small headcount.
