02
The Challenge

A dessert-led kitchen has roughly two seconds of attention from a scrolling viewer. The frame either earns the save or doesn't. Monet's plating could earn it — but only if the cinematography led with the plate, not the face. The challenge was getting 20 creators to follow that brief.

  • Plate first, face second. The cinematic grammar had to be food-as-portrait, not creator-with-food. We rewrote the standard creator brief to enforce it across 20 creators.
  • Actor anchor logic. Yashvi Bagga's recommendation reads as a friend's — but only if the post doesn't read as a paid integration. The brief avoided campaign markers.
  • Bollywood audio bed. Rahat Fateh Ali Khan's track gave Monet a Hindi-pop mood — opening up an audience pool that English-only creators don't reach.
  • Volume that converts. 20 creators is a saturation roster. To avoid 'campaign fatigue', we sequenced visits across multiple weeks and varied formats.
